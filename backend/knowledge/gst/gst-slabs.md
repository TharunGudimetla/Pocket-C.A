# GST Rate Slabs & Tax Rules in India

## Category
GST (Goods and Services Tax)

## Keywords
GST, rate slabs, tax rates, CGST, SGST, IGST, 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, GST India

## Summary
Overview of GST tax slabs in India, tax structures (CGST, SGST, IGST), and category classification.

## Detailed Content
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a single indirect tax levied on the sale of goods and services in India, replacing older taxes such as Central Excise, VAT, and Service Tax.

### GST Tax Slabs in India
1. **0% (Nil Rate / Exempt)**: Essential food items, fresh vegetables, unbranded grains, milk, salt, healthcare, and educational services.
2. **5% Rate**: Essential items including packaged food, tea, coffee, edible oil, domestic LPG, and economy air travel.
3. **12% Rate**: Processed food items, apparel above ₹1000, computers, business class air tickets, and medical equipment.
4. **18% Rate**: Standard rate applicable to IT services, telecom, financial services, consumer goods, capital goods, and restaurants.
5. **28% Rate**: Luxury goods, demerit items, automobiles, aerated drinks, tobacco products, and online gaming.

### Tax Dual Structure
- **CGST (Central GST)**: Collected by the Central Government on intra-state sales.
- **SGST (State GST)**: Collected by the State Government on intra-state sales.
- **IGST (Integrated GST)**: Collected by the Central Government on inter-state sales and imports, split between Centre and destination State.
