# Input Tax Credit (ITC) Rules under Section 16 of GST Act

## Category
GST (Goods and Services Tax)

## Keywords
Input Tax Credit, ITC, Section 16, GSTR-2B, GSTR-3B, tax credit, invoice matching, GST return

## Summary
Complete guidelines for claiming Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Section 16 of the CGST Act 2017.

## Detailed Content
Input Tax Credit (ITC) allows a registered taxpayer to reduce the tax paid on business purchases (inputs) from the tax liability collected on sales (outputs).

### Conditions to Claim ITC (Section 16)
1. **Tax Invoice**: The buyer must possess a valid tax invoice or debit note issued by a registered supplier.
2. **Receipt of Goods/Services**: The goods or services must have been received by the taxpayer.
3. **Tax Paid by Supplier**: The supplier must have paid the tax charged to the government either in cash or through eligible ITC.
4. **Filing of Returns**: The recipient must have filed the GSTR-3B return.
5. **GSTR-2B Matching**: Under Section 16(2)(aa), the invoice details must be reflected in the buyer's auto-generated GSTR-2B statement.

### Ineligible & Blocked Credits (Section 17(5))
ITC cannot be claimed on:
- Motor vehicles for transportation of persons (except for specific business uses).
- Food, beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, and health services.
- Goods or services used for personal consumption.
- Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, or written off.
