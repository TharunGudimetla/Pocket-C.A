# GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B Return Filing Rules

## Category
GST (Goods and Services Tax)

## Keywords
GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B, GST return, outward supplies, tax return filing, due dates

## Summary
Explanation of key monthly and quarterly GST returns in India, including GSTR-1, GSTR-2B, and GSTR-3B.

## Detailed Content
GST return filing is a mandatory periodic compliance for all registered businesses in India to report sales, purchases, output tax liability, and input tax credit claimed.

### GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies Return)
- **Purpose**: Details of all sales (b2b invoices, b2c sales, exports, debit/credit notes).
- **Due Date**: 11th of the following month for monthly filers; 13th of the month following the quarter for QRMP scheme filers.
- **Key Note**: No tax payment is made in GSTR-1; it is purely an informational return.

### GSTR-2B (Auto-Generated ITC Statement)
- **Purpose**: Static statement generated on the 14th of every month showing eligible and ineligible ITC available to the buyer based on suppliers' GSTR-1 filings.

### GSTR-3B (Summary Return & Tax Payment)
- **Purpose**: Summary return to declare total sales, eligible ITC, and pay net tax liability in cash or credit.
- **Due Date**: 20th of the following month for monthly filers (or 22nd/24th depending on state for QRMP filers).
- **Late Fee**: ₹50 per day (₹20 per day for Nil return filers) plus interest @ 18% per annum on net cash tax liability paid late under Section 50.
