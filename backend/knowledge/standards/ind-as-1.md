# Ind AS 1 - Presentation of Financial Statements

## Category
Ind AS & Accounting Standards

## Keywords
Ind AS 1, AS 1, financial statements, balance sheet, profit and loss, true and fair view, going concern, accrual basis

## Summary
Core principles and structure for preparing and presenting financial statements under Indian Accounting Standard 1.

## Detailed Content
Ind AS 1 sets out overall requirements for the presentation of financial statements, guidelines for their structure, and minimum requirements for their content.

### Complete Set of Financial Statements
1. Balance Sheet as at the end of the period.
2. Statement of Profit and Loss for the period.
3. Statement of Changes in Equity for the period.
4. Statement of Cash Flows for the period.
5. Notes, including significant accounting policies and explanatory information.

### Fundamental Accounting Principles
- **Going Concern**: Financial statements prepared on assumption that entity will continue operations in foreseeable future.
- **Accrual Basis of Accounting**: Items recognized as assets, liabilities, equity, income, and expenses when they satisfy definitions and recognition criteria.
- **Materiality & Aggregation**: Present separately each material class of similar items.
