# Ind AS 7 - Statement of Cash Flows

## Category
Ind AS & Accounting Standards

## Keywords
Ind AS 7, cash flow statement, operating activities, investing activities, financing activities, cash equivalents

## Summary
Classification and preparation guidelines for Statement of Cash Flows under Ind AS 7.

## Detailed Content
Ind AS 7 requires entities to provide information about historical changes in cash and cash equivalents by means of a statement of cash flows.

### Three Categories of Cash Flow
1. **Operating Activities**: Principal revenue-producing activities (cash receipts from customers, cash paid to suppliers and employees).
2. **Investing Activities**: Acquisition and disposal of long-term assets and investments (purchase of machinery, sale of property, interest received).
3. **Financing Activities**: Activities resulting in changes in equity and borrowing structure (issuance of shares, repayment of loans, dividends paid).
