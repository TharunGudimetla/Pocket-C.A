# Ind AS 2 - Valuation of Inventories

## Category
Ind AS & Accounting Standards

## Keywords
Ind AS 2, inventory valuation, cost or NRV, net realizable value, FIFO, weighted average cost

## Summary
Accounting treatment and valuation principles for inventories under Ind AS 2.

## Detailed Content
Ind AS 2 prescribes the accounting treatment for inventories, primarily the determination of cost and its subsequent recognition as an expense.

### Valuation Rule
- Inventories shall be measured at the **Lower of Cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV)**.

### Cost Formulas Allowed
- **FIFO (First-In, First-Out)**
- **Weighted Average Cost Formula**
*(Note: LIFO formula is strictly prohibited under Ind AS 2).*
