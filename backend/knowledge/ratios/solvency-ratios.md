# Solvency Ratios - Debt to Equity & Interest Coverage Ratio

## Category
Financial Ratios & Metrics

## Keywords
solvency ratio, debt to equity, interest coverage ratio, leverage ratio, total debt, shareholder equity

## Summary
Analysis of long-term solvency ratios to assess financial leverage and debt obligations.

## Detailed Content
Solvency ratios evaluate a company's ability to meet long-term debt and interest obligations over an extended period.

### 1. Debt-to-Equity (D/E) Ratio
- **Formula**: `Debt to Equity = Total Debt / Shareholder Equity`
- **Interpretation**: Measures proportion of debt financing relative to equity financing.
- **Benchmark**: A ratio of **1:1 to 2:1** is generally considered healthy for industrial firms.

### 2. Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR)
- **Formula**: `Interest Coverage = EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Tax) / Interest Expense`
- **Benchmark**: A ratio above **3.0** indicates strong ability to service debt interest payments.
