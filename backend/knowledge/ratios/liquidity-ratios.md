# Liquidity Ratios - Current Ratio & Quick Ratio

## Category
Financial Ratios & Metrics

## Keywords
liquidity ratio, current ratio, quick ratio, acid test ratio, working capital, current assets, current liabilities

## Summary
Formulas, benchmarks, and interpretation for Current Ratio and Quick (Acid-Test) Ratio.

## Detailed Content
Liquidity ratios measure a company's ability to cover its short-term obligations using its short-term assets.

### 1. Current Ratio
- **Formula**: `Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities`
- **Current Assets**: Cash, bank balances, short-term investments, trade receivables, inventory.
- **Current Liabilities**: Trade payables, short-term loans, accrued expenses.
- **Benchmark**: Ideal ratio is **2.0 : 1** (or 2:1).

### 2. Quick Ratio (Acid-Test Ratio)
- **Formula**: `Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid Expenses) / Current Liabilities`
- **Interpretation**: Measures immediate liquidity without relying on selling inventory.
- **Benchmark**: Ideal ratio is **1.0 : 1** (or 1:1).
