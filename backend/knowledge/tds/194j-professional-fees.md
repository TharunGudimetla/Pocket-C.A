# Section 194J - TDS on Professional and Technical Fees

## Category
TDS & TCS Sections

## Keywords
Section 194J, 194J, TDS on professional fees, technical services, royalty, director fees, 10%, 2%, threshold limit

## Summary
Complete legal rules, threshold limits, and rates for deducting TDS under Section 194J of Income Tax Act 1961.

## Detailed Content
Section 194J requires any person (other than individuals/HUF not subject to tax audit) making payments to a resident for specified services to deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS).

### Applicable Payment Types & Rates
1. **Professional Services**: 10% TDS (CA fees, legal fees, medical, architectural, management consulting).
2. **Technical Services**: 2% TDS (IT services, engineering, software maintenance, technical support).
3. **Royalty for Computer Software**: 2% TDS.
4. **Director Fees / Remuneration**: 10% TDS (no threshold limit applies to director fees).
5. **Non-Compete Fees**: 10% TDS.

### Threshold Limit
- TDS under Sec 194J applies if the total amount paid or credited to a single payee exceeds **₹30,000** in a financial year for each separate category of service.

### Due Date & Form 16A
- **Deposit Due Date**: 7th of the following month (30th April for March deductions).
- **Quarterly Return**: Form 26Q filed quarterly.
- **Certificate**: Form 16A issued quarterly to payee.
