# Section 194C - TDS on Payments to Contractors & Sub-contractors

## Category
TDS & TCS Sections

## Keywords
Section 194C, 194C, TDS contractor, sub-contractor, job work, transport contract, 1%, 2%, threshold limit

## Summary
Legal framework, rates, and thresholds for deducting tax at source on payments to contractors under Section 194C.

## Detailed Content
Section 194C of the Income Tax Act governs TDS deduction on payments made for carrying out any work (including supply of labor for carrying out any work) in pursuance of a contract.

### Applicable Rates
- **1% TDS**: Where payment is made to an **Individual or HUF contractor**.
- **2% TDS**: Where payment is made to a **Company, Partnership Firm, LLP, or other entities**.
- **0% (Exemption for Transport Contractors)**: No TDS is deducted if payment is made to a goods transport operator owning 10 or fewer goods carriages during the year and providing a valid PAN declaration.

### Threshold Limits
- **Single Payment**: If a single bill/contract payment exceeds **₹30,000**.
- **Aggregate Annual Limit**: If total aggregate payments to a contractor exceed **₹1,00,000** in a financial year.

## Source Reference
