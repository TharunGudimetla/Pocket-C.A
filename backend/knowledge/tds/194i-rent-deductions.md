# Section 194I - TDS on Rent Payments

## Category
TDS & TCS Sections

## Keywords
Section 194I, 194I, TDS on rent, building rent, plant machinery rent, land rent, 2%, 10%, threshold ₹2,40,000

## Summary
Legal requirements and rates for TDS deduction on rent under Section 194I of Income Tax Act 1961.

## Detailed Content
Section 194I governs tax deduction on rent paid for use of land, building, machinery, plant, equipment, furniture, or fittings.

### Applicable Rates
1. **2% TDS**: For rent paid for use of **Plant, Machinery, or Equipment**.
2. **10% TDS**: For rent paid for use of **Land, Building (including factory building), Furniture, or Fittings**.

### Threshold Limit
- TDS is applicable if total rent paid or payable during the financial year exceeds **₹2,40,000** per landlord/property.
